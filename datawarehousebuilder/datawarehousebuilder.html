What I Do

I focus on practical engineering work rather than adding unnecessary layers, tools or infrastructure. The goal is a data platform that works, performs and can be maintained.

Warehouse Architecture

Design, modernization, dimensional modeling, schema design and architecture for analytical and operational data platforms.

Data Integration

Bring together internal systems, APIs, public data, geographic data and third-party sources into coherent, curated datasets.

Performance & Cost

Diagnose inefficient SQL, models and pipelines before throwing more hardware or cloud spend at the problem.

ETL / ELT Engineering

Build and repair ingestion, transformation and orchestration pipelines using SQL, Python, DBT and Airflow.

Migration & Modernization

Move legacy or fragmented warehouse environments toward modern PostgreSQL, Snowflake and cloud-oriented architectures.

Data Enrichment & Analytics

Clean, standardize, enrich and analyze customer data using geographic, demographic, property, environmental and other external datasets.
